Intelligence is racing toward free. The durable advantage is no longer access to answers — it's iteration speed and the quality of the feedback loop.

If you can't tell within an hour whether the thing you just built is wrong, a smarter model doesn't save you. It just gets you to the wrong place sooner and with more confidence.

That is why this site stays sparse on day one. I would rather ship a quiet stream and backfill with real notes than invent a feed that looks busy.
