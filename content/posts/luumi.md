An adorable animated native Mac assistant. Just a small, slightly sassy cat.

Luumi can use an AI gateway or local Ollama, with explicit actions for Notes, Calendar, and Music.

But mostly i just like how she sits in the way of everything and purrs when i pick her up to move her.
