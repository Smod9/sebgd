I built a monitoring app for Bon Vivant’s DC electrical system. It brings Victron SmartShunt readings and the Renogy DCC50S charger into one dashboard, with live updates and history.

It runs on a Mac or Raspberry Pi, with a mobile-friendly dashboard and a Capacitor iOS shell.

The wider refit and logbook live in [The boat](/work/bon-vivant).
